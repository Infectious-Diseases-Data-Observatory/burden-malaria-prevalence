# Issues to resolve before a final analysis

Updated 7 September 2026. The current complete-case fit is an exploratory implementation of the [seven-band model](PFPR_AGE_BAND_MODEL.md). These issues remain open; fitting the model does not resolve them.

## Decisions for the current trial

- Seven completed-month bands: <1, 1–5, 6–11, 12–23, 24–35, 36–47 and 48–59.
- Entry during the 60 months before interview, with the full potential band ending by interview for both deaths and survivors.
- PfPR and annual external covariates assigned at band-entry year.
- Complete cases for the actual model variables, with no imputation. As requested, vaccine covariates are excluded from this trial.
- Adjustment set: sex, multiple birth, birth order, maternal age at birth, maternal education, household wealth quintile, urban residence, log child HIV prevalence, log GDP per capita, log health expenditure per capita and political stability. Effects vary by age band. Wealth is categorical; continuous confounders enter linearly after centering/scaling.
- Separate PfPR and calendar-time smooths per age band, plus survey, country-by-age and survey-versioned region random intercepts. Use the proposed unweighted conditional binomial likelihood with cloglog link and full-band-width offset. A survey-weighted final specification has not been chosen.

The pre-fit selection retains **5,261,313 child-band records and 69,772 deaths**, in **33 countries, 102 surveys and 998 survey-specific regions**. Complete cases refer to model inputs, not every optional field in the dataset. Records with missing unused vaccines or stratum metadata can remain in this trial.

## Data coverage and measurement

| Issue | Evidence / implication | Work needed | Current trial treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HIV coverage** | 774,565 of 6,431,363 eligible records (12.04%) lack HIV. Nigeria, Comoros and São Tomé and Príncipe have no rows in the derived child-HIV panel. Direct inspection and an R re-import confirmed that Nigeria has 378 rows in the original workbook, all ages 15–19; its required ages 0–14 series is absent before any merge. Population denominators exist for all 25 years. | Locate a suitable ages 0–14 HIV series, verify country/age/sex/year definitions and validate its extraction. Do not infer that all UNAIDS sources lack Nigeria merely because this UNICEF workbook does. | All three countries are excluded from this complete-case fit. No adolescent substitution or imputation. |
| **HIV covariate definition** | The current measure is estimated ages 0–14 prevalence, not prevalence in each mortality band. ART/PMTCT can change mortality independently of prevalence. The cached extraction uses midpoints for values such as `<500`. | Review prevalence versus paediatric HIV mortality/treatment covariates, censored-estimate handling and available uncertainty. | Use the positive cached prevalence on the natural-log scale. |
| **Vaccine coverage** | Missingness is 25.21% for Hib3, 58.17% for PCV and 70.42% for rotavirus after rejecting assumed pre-series/no-series zeros. Adding all three to the proposed covariates would retain 1,825,229 rows in 24 countries and 41 surveys. | Distinguish verified non-introduction from unavailable reporting and gaps. Decide which vaccine covariates belong in the adjustment set and how to recover valid historical coverage. | Excluded at the user's request, rather than imputed or assigned zero. |
| **Other covariates and complete-case selection** | Health expenditure is missing in 0.98%, political stability in 2.83%, wealth in 1.79% and maternal education in 0.05% of eligible rows. Entire surveys/countries can be lost. Complete-case analysis can change the target population and introduce selection bias. | Examine exclusion patterns by country, year, PfPR and age; distinguish unavailable years/fields from merge problems. Specify a missing-data strategy and assess robustness. | Require all eleven selected covariates; save per-survey selection and overlapping per-variable missing counts. |
| **Unresolved geographic matches** | 28 source labels in 17 surveys remain unmatched, affecting 71,904 eligible rows. Some are historical groupings or labels explicitly excluding cities; others are absent from the MAP cache. | Verify recode-to-polygon definitions and add justified mappings. Do not remove exclusion clauses or infer matches by elimination. | Retain audit records; exclude unmatched rows from fitting. |
| **MAP coverage and exact annual joins** | Four Lesotho surveys lack MAP geography entirely. Another 1,657 eligible rows have a known region but no exact annual PfPR match. Missing coverage can be concentrated in low-transmission areas. | Check extraction coverage, raster no-data versus genuine zero prevalence, and available years. | No zero substitution, interpolation or nearest-year borrowing. |
| **Regional identity across surveys** | Current region identifiers include survey/boundary version. Similar names do not establish equivalent areas. | Harmonize stable boundaries or specify how changed areas enter a longitudinal model. Assess dependence on between-region comparisons. | Region intercepts are survey-specific; they do not represent a single stable regional effect across surveys. |
| **Measurement timing and migration** | Annual exposures summarize the entry year. Household wealth, urban residence and education are measured at interview; location is also residence at interview. Historical residence is unknown. | Review temporal ordering and availability of residence histories. Consider whether survey-time variables are confounders, proxies or consequences of exposure/outcome. | Keep the specified entry-year annual assignment; use survey-time individual covariates provisionally. |
| **Calendar and death-age coding** | Older Ethiopian recodes use an approximate +92 CMC adjustment; ET8AFL is Gregorian. Month-based neonatal deaths include reported days 0–30, whereas Burstein's text uses a 28-day boundary. The build excluded 38 ambiguous death-band histories, 696 impossible death/interview timings and 1,810 invalid weights. | Verify calendar conversions, age-reporting/imputation flags, B6/B7 disagreements and sensitivity to neonatal/interval definitions. | Preserve provenance flags and current explicit rules; no fabricated exact death dates. |
| **Partial follow-up and earliest years** | The entry window includes 994,618 band opportunities that are incomplete by interview. Another 418,661 complete-band opportunities fall outside the configured 2000–2024 entry years. | Assess information loss and target-population consequences. Consider an interval-censored or appropriately timed person-time model that uses partial follow-up. | Apply the same full-potential-band eligibility rule to deaths and survivors. |

## Statistical specification and inference

| Issue | Work needed | Current trial treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **Final causal adjustment set** | Review a causal structure for malaria, HIV, development, health services and household conditions. Resolve treatment of WASH, wasting, vaccination, birth spacing and possible mediators. Assess nonlinear confounder relationships and interactions. | The eleven-variable set above is a provisional agreed trial, not a validated sufficient adjustment set. WASH/wasting and preceding-birth interval are not included; the latter has structural missingness for first births. |
| **Survey weights and uncertainty** | Choose a weighting/target-population convention and a method accounting for sampling, mother/child dependence and PSU clustering. Validate strata definitions; UG61FL lacks strata for all 44,636 eligible rows. | Unweighted likelihood with survey and geographic random effects. These do not replace a survey-design variance estimator. Any reported intervals are approximate model-based intervals. |
| **Spline complexity, confounding and fit** | Check convergence, rank, age-specific support, smooth EDF/basis adequacy, calibration and concurvity between PfPR, time and development covariates. Refit suspect basis dimensions and assess geographic/temporal robustness. | Initial cubic regression spline dimensions are k=5 for PfPR and k=6 for time per band, estimated by discrete `mgcv::bam` with fREML. All selected complete cases enter the fit. |
| **Likelihood and rate interpretation** | Assess the constant within-band hazard assumption, dependence and residual heterogeneity; compare a logit or appropriate event-time specification if warranted. | Bernoulli band-death outcomes with cloglog link. The offset is the predetermined band width, not observed time to death. Hazard ratios and band-death probability ratios are distinct. |
| **Causal contrast and survivor selection** | Specify supported PfPR changes and target populations. A change from birth can alter who reaches later bands, so conditional age-band effects are not automatically total life-course effects. | A 40% to 20% PfPR contrast is evaluated within each age band, holding other predictors fixed. This remains an adjusted model association pending causal validation. |
| **Exposure uncertainty and extrapolation** | Assess MAP uncertainty, aggregation and support, and carry uncertainty through any eventual burden calculation. Plan sensitivity analyses before selecting results. | Use point-estimate regional PfPR. No burden extrapolation or claim of final causal effects in this trial. |

## Pipeline work still needed

The [first completed fit](../results/cbh/age_band_complete_case_v1/REPORT.md) reports convergence and full rank, but adds two concrete diagnostic follow-ups: increase/check the PfPR basis (older-band EDF reaches 3.86 out of a maximum 4), and check smoothing-parameter stability (the optimization Hessian has a small negative eigenvalue despite the convergence flags). The repeated PfPR k-index across factor-by terms is a pooled diagnostic, not seven independent age-specific tests. Model-based intervals remain provisional.

1. Migrate raw ZIP/registry ingestion, MAP extraction and external-data acquisition into the new pipeline. Stage 1 currently depends on local extraction snapshots, although it no longer sources legacy fitting scripts.
2. Resolve the source and mapping issues above, rebuild under explicit provenance, and compare included countries/years and mortality counts with the current snapshot.
3. Implement the agreed weighting/uncertainty method and planned sensitivity fits, followed by final plotting/reporting. Keep complete-case selection, model specification, cached fit and figure provenance aligned.
