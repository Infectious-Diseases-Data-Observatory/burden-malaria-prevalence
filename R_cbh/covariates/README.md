# Primary regional adjustment

The latest 17 September 2026 primary specification uses **18 regional/annual scalar confounders**. **Urban/rural residence remains required as `urban_pct`**, a weighted survey-region percentage. Hib3, PCV, rotavirus and exclusive breastfeeding are excluded from both the formula and complete-case selection. All DHS confounders are survey-region means or percentages, not individual predictors. National annual inputs remain constant across regions within a country-year. The child-band outcome, MAP exposure at entry, time spline, gamma=2 and seven separate age-band models are unchanged.

This stage builds and audits a covariate overlay. It does **not** refit mortality models or modify existing base shards, fitted objects, manuscript figures or TeX. `run_all.R --primary` still reproduces the preceding individual-adjustment benchmark. A new fitting version and prepared dataset are needed before revised results can be promoted.

**Current primary policy:** retain usable regional vaccination estimates and replace missing DTP3/measles coverage with exact country/survey-year UNICEF WUENIC estimates. Fill other missing regional summaries with the arithmetic mean of available regions in the same survey, separately by variable; preserve observed values and leave whole-survey gaps unresolved. The current 17-variable availability overlay is `planned17_audit`, with [missingness results](../../results/cbh/planned17_covariate_missingness/README.md). Scripts 11–13 extract maternal age at first birth, fetch WHO-standard regional wasting/stunting and audit the new set; script 14 attributes each excluded MAP-eligible record to its first missing covariate (national series first, then regional summaries) so the study-flow diagram can show disjoint exclusion counts (`results/cbh/planned17_covariate_missingness/EXCLUSION_ATTRIBUTION.md`); `14_exclusion_attribution.R --liberia` (24 September 2026) repeats it with the HIV panel that adds Liberia's UNAIDS-derived child incidence, for the DHS part of the v7 primary, writing `results/cbh/planned17_covariate_missingness_lbr/`. Scripts 15-16 impute the remaining gaps for the **imputed-covariate sensitivity version** (`primary_map_regional17_imputed_gamma2_v4`): 15 fills the national series (2001 WGI interpolation; health-expenditure GAM for Zimbabwe 2000-2009 and 2024), 16 runs chained-equation multiple imputation (`mice`, pmm, m = 10) over the survey-region overlay; outputs under `data/derived_cbh/regional_adjustment/imputed_v4/` and audits in `results/cbh/covariate_imputation_v4/`. The complete-case `planned17_audit` overlay used by the primary is unchanged. The previous 18-variable overlay/audit `regional_adjustment_reduced_v3` is preserved; scripts 01–10 reproduce that earlier policy. Model preparation uses `R_cbh/primary/specification.R` and the audited new overlay. The 22-variable UNICEF v2 and no-substitution v1 audits remain preserved. Pre-rollout zeros for the three dropped vaccines are irrelevant to the reduced primary selection.

Run the local pipeline from the project root with `Rscript R_cbh/covariates/run.R`. It extracts/caches recode summaries, reads the existing UNICEF snapshot, applies national and regional-mean substitution and regenerates availability reports and validation. Public DHS retrieval remains a separate explicit command. `--legacy-expanded` reproduces the 22-variable UNICEF v2 policy; `--complete-case-only` reproduces the earlier 22-variable no-substitution policy. Source extraction can retain excluded historical fields; the reduced wide overlay and model selector omit them.

Individual stages:

```sh

# Add --refresh only for an intentional new source snapshot.
```

No packages are installed. The data retrieval sends only public survey/indicator IDs. It retains original API records, indicator definitions, denominators, recall windows, geographic labels, URLs and hashes under `data/derived_cbh/regional_adjustment/`. The DHS API's `DataId` is not globally unique; semantic/source keys are checked instead. Published estimates can be repeated at nested geographic levels; equal values with equal denominators can be deduplicated, conflicting values remain missing unless a reviewed source-version rule resolves them.

## Definitions and joins

See [analysis plan Section 2.4](../../docs/ANALYSIS_PLAN.md) for the complete covariate dictionary. `regional.R` defines the selected variables and the new formula through `cbh_regional_spec()` and `cbh_regional_formula()`.

- Sex, multiple birth, birth order and maternal age: women's-weighted summaries over live births in the 60 months before interview, counting births once. This population is independent of survival, complete-band eligibility and exposure availability.
- Education, mean wealth-quintile score and urban residence: women's-weighted summaries over distinct interviewed mothers with a birth in that interval. These are **not all-household averages**. Wealth is an ordinal 1–5 mean, replacing the former individual categorical predictor.
- Regional DTP3/measles, delivery, WASH and electricity: published DHS estimates. Household denominators/weights come from the published source, avoiding the legacy practice of applying women's weights to deduplicated BR households. Delivery uses five-year estimates where available, otherwise an explicitly flagged three-/two-year window. The vaccination age window follows the published indicator, normally 12–23 months; source-specific windows require checking when comparing releases.
- Short birth interval: sum published 7–17- and 18–23-month percentages only when their denominators agree. The denominator excludes first births; first births remain eligible mortality observations.
- Exclusive breastfeeding (historical only; excluded from current primary model): prefer published regional `CN_BFSS_C_EBF`; otherwise compute a survey-weighted estimate among youngest children aged 0–5 months living with their mother. Use B19 where available, otherwise V008−B3, youngest-child selection by BIDX, and child-specific M4. Never repeat maternal V404 across children. Require observed feeding domains and distinguish missing/don't-know answers from “no”. Survey-wide all-NA placeholders are not active questions. The implemented conservative screen flags a survey for questionnaire review when its recode national estimate differs from published `CN_IYCB_C_EXB` by more than 5 percentage points; it does not calibrate estimates to match. Published regional estimates remain usable in those surveys. A difference below this threshold is **not proof of full questionnaire equivalence**. Conditional skips and subsampling still need review where flagged, and unsupported estimates remain unavailable in this audit.
- HIV incidence, GDP, health expenditure and political stability: retain exact country/band-entry-year assignment from the base pipeline (with the same fixed child-incidence imputation). Hib3/PCV/rotavirus remain source-history fields only and are excluded from the current primary model.

Published source names retain exclusion/inclusion clauses. Only date qualifiers are normalized, and `published_region_versions.csv` explicitly selects old/new geographies for DRC, Ghana, Senegal, Sierra Leone and Uganda. `published_region_overrides.csv` documents capital-city inclusion rules. Reviewed aliases are explicit in `published_region_aliases.csv`. No fuzzy matching or averaging of overlapping regions is used. After source assembly, missing DTP3/measles values receive the declared national UNICEF fallback; remaining regional gaps then receive the available-region mean fallback. A directly reported broad-region estimate takes precedence over fine units mapped to that broad region.

The current wide overlay is `data/derived_cbh/regional_adjustment/reduced_v3/regional_covariates_wide.csv` and has one row per `(survey, regkey)`. It includes `*_before_imputation`, `*_imputed`, `*_source_year` and `*_imputation_source` columns for DTP3/measles. `cbh_unicef_fill()` in `unicef.R` performs exact-year substitution and preserves observed values; use survey year for these retained regional vaccination measures. The current overlay additionally includes `*_before_regional_mean`, `*_regional_mean_imputed`, `*_donor_regions` and `*_regional_mean_source` for the 14 regional predictors. The versioned manifest records the policy, source hashes and base dataset manifest. National fallback uncertainty is not propagated; retain an observed-regional-only sensitivity.

The wide overlay has one row per `(survey, regkey)`. Join it to **all eligible base shards**, then attach child HIV incidence and apply complete-case selection on `cbh_regional_spec()$covariates`. Do not start by restricting to the previous complete-case sample: regional means can recover records with missing individual answers. All 18 predictors are then centered/scaled using newly saved preprocessing. No child-level confounder terms belong in the new formula.

## Audit outputs and limits

`08_missingness_time_after_imputation.R` writes `missingness_by_entry_year.csv` and `missingness_by_period.csv` in the historical `regional_adjustment_unicef_v2` directory. It deliberately preserves the historical 22-variable specification and compares all 22 covariates and joint missingness before/after UNICEF substitution on the same previous-primary sample, reconciling totals with both availability audits. Percentages count child–age-band records, not distinct children or regions; national vaccine exposure years are band-entry years, while DTP3/measles substitution uses survey year. Years without eligible records are absent, not assigned zero missingness. Country composition changes over time.

`09_vaccine_introduction_history.py` summarises WHO's separate introduction-status history for 48 sub-Saharan African countries, distinguishing first partial and first nationwide reporting. It documents the Nigeria PCV discrepancy between the December 2014 launch and 2015 first annual partial-introduction record. This descriptive audit does not substitute zeros or alter any mortality input. See [introduction history](../../results/cbh/regional_adjustment_unicef_v2/VACCINE_INTRODUCTION_HISTORY.md).

`10_prenationwide_zero_scenario.R` preserves the historical 22-variable definition and evaluates the user-requested scenario of filling only missing Hib3/PCV/rotavirus values strictly before the first reported nationwide introduction year, including partial-rollout years. For countries still reported as not introduced, this extends through the last reported year. It preserves all observed values and leaves rollout-year gaps missing. Outputs under `prenationwide_zero_scenario/` include vaccine-only and all-covariate missingness, affected surveys/countries, and complete-case retention for both the previous-primary and full MAP-eligible samples. These historical scenarios do not change the preserved 18-variable policy or overwrite datasets/fits.

Add `--region-mean` to apply `cbh_regional_mean_fill()` to the 15 survey-region predictors, separately for each variable and survey, after the existing UNICEF substitution. It calculates arithmetic means on one row per region, omits nonfinite donors, preserves observed values and leaves all-missing surveys unresolved. The separate output directory is `prenationwide_zero_region_mean_scenario/`; its scope tables distinguish recoverable regional gaps from surveys without donors. The private overlay retains original values, imputation flags, donor counts and source labels. National country-year covariates cannot borrow from regions with the same missing national value. The original within-region BR aggregation already excludes nonfinite responses and invalid weights before calculating weighted means; individual NAs do not propagate into otherwise estimable regional means. Focused validation covers both aggregation levels.

[Previous 18-variable results](../../results/cbh/regional_adjustment_reduced_v3/README.md) ([historical expanded UNICEF audit](../../results/cbh/regional_adjustment_unicef_v2/README.md); [pre-substitution audit](../../results/cbh/regional_adjustment_v1/README.md)) include three distinct denominators: the previous primary sample, all MAP-eligible records, and the regional version of the previous adjustment set before the added covariates. “Lost region” means **zero** surviving child-band rows, while a partially reduced region retains at least one. Region identifiers are survey-specific, not unique areas across time. Multiple missing covariates overlap; do not sum their loss counts.

`recode_regional.csv` retains eligible/observed response counts, weighted denominators and flags for n<25/n<50. No automatic small-denominator exclusion was added. Published suppression may instead appear as an absent source estimate. The availability audit counts a mean as observed if its chosen source is usable; it does not assume every contributing respondent had a complete answer. It checks that the old selection reproduces 5,885,022 records, 82,415 deaths, 1,817,912 children and 1,015 survey-regions, without exporting respondent identifiers.

Before refitting, resolve or explicitly retain the remaining source/geography exclusions and review source-window comparability. The dropped vaccine and breastfeeding fields no longer exclude primary records. The regional averages also have sampling error; their current point estimates do not propagate that uncertainty.
