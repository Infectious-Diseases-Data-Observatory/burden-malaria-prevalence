Deaths before age five in 2024. Countries are ordered by the absolute difference between the PfPR-ACM model and IHME; the total always includes all 42 estimable countries. UN IGME is the direct under-five CA-CODE 2026 series, not a WHO all-age proxy. Model values use the revised 17-variable regional-adjustment MAP gamma=2 fits. Point estimates are rounded to the nearest death; totals are calculated before rounding. These sources estimate different quantities; their agreement is descriptive.
