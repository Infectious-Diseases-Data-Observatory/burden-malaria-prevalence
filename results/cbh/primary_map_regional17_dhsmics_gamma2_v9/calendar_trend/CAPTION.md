# Supplementary figure: calendar-year splines by age band

Calendar-year splines of the seven age-band models of the primary analysis (primary_map_regional17_dhsmics_gamma2_v9): the hazard ratio for entering the band in a given month relative to January 2000, conditional on PfPR[2–10] at band entry, the 17 covariates and the survey, country and survey-region random intercepts, with 95% intervals conditional on the estimated smoothing parameters. The curve is the secular change in all-cause mortality not explained by the other terms and is common to all countries; panel titles give its effective degrees of freedom (1 = linear on the log-hazard scale). Grey bars show the number of records entering the band in each calendar year (scaled within each panel); no records enter in 2001 because the World Bank political-stability indicator was not published for that year and the primary analysis uses complete cases. Hazard ratios for 2024 relative to 2000: <1 month 0.64 (0.55–0.73); 1-5 months 0.44 (0.36–0.54); 6-11 months 0.47 (0.37–0.59); 12-23 months 0.53 (0.40–0.70); 24-35 months 0.42 (0.33–0.53); 36-47 months 0.43 (0.33–0.54); 48-59 months 0.54 (0.41–0.72).
